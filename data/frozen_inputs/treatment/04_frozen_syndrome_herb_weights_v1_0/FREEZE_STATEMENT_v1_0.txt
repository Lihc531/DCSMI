Syndrome-Herb Evidence Weighting Rule v1.0
STATUS: FROZEN

Primary weight:
W_hs = (m_hs + 0.5) / (M_s + 1)

m_hs = number of independent eligible articles supporting herb h in syndrome s.
M_s  = number of independent eligible articles assigned to syndrome s.

Primary relations:
- composition evidence D
- syndrome tier A/B
- complete/available composition when fields are present
- explicit herb roles: base or conditional_addition
- conditional additions are not pre-downweighted
- unspecified herbs are never invented

FULL:
All eligible primary articles.

LOPO:
All pneumonia/CAP articles are excluded BEFORE evidence construction.
LOPO is the primary representation intended for later CAP molecular alignment.

Not used to define W:
- GAT/GCN
- CAP transcriptomic results
- syndrome-specificity multiplier
- prescription frequency as the independent evidence unit
