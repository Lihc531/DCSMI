PHASE 12A — DEGREE-PRESERVING HERB–TARGET CONVERGENCE NULL v1.0
FINAL FREEZE STATEMENT
Freeze date: 2026-09-22
Status: FINAL / CLOSED / FROZEN

FROZEN QUESTION
Does the convergence of the three LOPO ubiquity-corrected syndrome target profiles exceed what is expected solely from the exact herb- and target-degree structure of the frozen TCM-ID bipartite graph?

FROZEN PRIMARY TEST
Primary statistic: generalized Jensen-Shannon divergence (GJSD; log base 2).
Direction: lower GJSD = greater convergence.
Primary alternative: observed GJSD lower than the exact degree-preserving null.
Null: Curveball randomization of the full 172-herb x 374-target graph, preserving every herb degree, every target degree, and all 13,285 edges.
N null graphs: 10,000, 4 chains x 2,500.

FINAL PRIMARY RESULT
Observed GJSD = 0.0527603500918
Null mean = 0.0401465307726
Null SD = 0.00333729990673
Z vs null = +3.77964812025
Primary lower-tail empirical P = 0.997200279972

FINAL INTERPRETATION
The prespecified extra-convergence hypothesis is NOT supported.
Observed syndrome target profiles are not more convergent than expected from exact bipartite degree structure. They are instead more divergent than the degree-preserving null expectation (diagnostic upper-tail P=0.002899710029; diagnostic two-sided P=0.005799420058).
Common-mass and mean-pairwise-JSD diagnostics point in the same direction.

SCIENTIFIC CONSEQUENCE
The high absolute overlap among the three observed syndrome target profiles must not be interpreted as evidence for a shared molecular architecture beyond database topology. Exact herb/target degree structure alone generates even stronger convergence under random reassignment. The observed herb-target assignments retain additional syndrome differentiation relative to that structural null.
This result does not address whether any shared component is CAP-associated; that is a separate disease-context question for a separately prespecified downstream analysis.

FROZEN POLICY
1. Do not alter the Phase 12A null model, GJSD primary statistic, test direction, randomization parameters, or upstream representation after seeing this result.
2. Do not relabel the opposite-direction diagnostic as support for the original convergence hypothesis.
3. Do not claim that Phase 12A validates a CAP shared disease backbone.
4. Phase 12A may be used to motivate a refined model: substantial absolute target overlap coexists with herb-target assignments that preserve more syndrome differentiation than expected from degree structure alone.
5. Any alternative null or convergence statistic requires a new version and must be justified independently of the present result.
6. Phase 12B/C must be prespecified separately and may not retroactively modify Phase 12A.

PHASE 12A v1.0 IS CLOSED AND FROZEN.
