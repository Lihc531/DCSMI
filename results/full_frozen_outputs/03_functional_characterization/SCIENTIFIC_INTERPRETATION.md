# Scientific interpretation

The frozen positive-anchor criterion was not met. Across 1,560 eligible GO-BP terms (4,680 syndrome-term primary differential tests) and 207 eligible KEGG pathways (621 tests), no term/pathway had Delta > 0 with BH-FDR q < 0.05. Minimum family q values were 0.799754 (GO-BP) and 0.829396 (KEGG). Specific-only sensitivity also yielded zero significant differential terms, and the shared component yielded zero structurally calibrated annotation terms at q < 0.05.

This result does not show that prescriptions lack annotated biological functions. It shows that, under the frozen 374-target universe and degree x ubiquity matched structural null, the weighted GO-BP/KEGG organization does not provide multiplicity-controlled evidence for syndrome-associated functional differentiation beyond target-database structure. Nominal pathways/terms must not be promoted to mechanistic claims.

Manuscript consequence: the predeclared functional-analysis positive anchor is absent. The defensible paper direction is therefore narrower: structural-bias diagnosis plus evidence-boundary/falsification methodology, rather than syndrome-specific functional architecture or patient syndrome mechanism.
