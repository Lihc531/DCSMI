DCSMI URTI B1 — TWO-SYNDROME P3 AMENDMENT v1.0
=================================================

STATUS: FROZEN_PRE_RESULT_METHOD_AMENDMENT
URTI endpoint results inspected: NO
Scope: URTI P3 only.

1. IDENTIFIABILITY PROBLEM
--------------------------
For a two-group direct contrast:

    Z(WC vs WH) = - Z(WH vs WC)

The frozen observed-state construction is direction-agnostic and uses |Zobs|.
Therefore:

    |Z(WC vs WH)| = |Z(WH vs WC)|

and hence the two nominal syndrome observed profiles are identical:

    O_WH = O_WC = O

The originally intended own-versus-other selectivity endpoint,

    C_s = rho(M_s, O_s) - rho(M_s, O_other),

therefore gives:

    C_WH = 0
    C_WC = 0

by construction, independent of biology or data quality.

2. PRE-RESULT DECISION
----------------------
URTI P3 is designated:

    NOT_IDENTIFIABLE / NOT_TESTED

It must be reported as NA, not as a negative biological result.

No replacement primary P3 is introduced.

Rationale:
A signed or direction-specific replacement would change the frozen observed-state
estimand (|Z|-based direction-agnostic O) and would therefore constitute a new
scientific endpoint rather than a minimal code repair. Such an endpoint can be
developed prospectively in a future validation dataset, but not retrofitted here.

3. WHAT REMAINS UNCHANGED
-------------------------
- WH/WC syndrome definitions
- T_WH and T_WC
- primary disease-background selection
- |Zobs|-based observed-state construction
- Hallmark space
- P1
- P2
- structural-null principles
- seed / planned replicate counts
- multiplicity principle

4. OPTIONAL SECONDARY DESCRIPTIVE QUANTITY
------------------------------------------
After primary execution, the following may be shown descriptively:

    prediction_separation =
        rho(M_WH, O) - rho(M_WC, O)

This is NOT P3.
It is NOT a confirmatory endpoint.
It must NOT be used to rescue primary syndrome-specificity claims.
It must NOT enter the primary P3 Holm family.

5. INTERPRETATION
-----------------
Psoriasis (three syndromes) remains capable of testing the prespecified
own-versus-other P3 selectivity construct.

URTI (two syndromes, one direction-agnostic direct contrast) can test patient
correspondence through P1 and disease-conditioning gain through P2, but cannot
identify the same own-versus-other P3 selectivity estimand.

This limitation should be reported explicitly as a design property, not hidden
and not interpreted as evidence that WH and WC are biologically identical.
